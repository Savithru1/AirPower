## 2. A2 Pre-maintenance risk (NGAFID, real flights)

Task: was a flight flown in the two days before or after an unplanned maintenance? NGAFID 2-day subset (11,446 real flights, Cessna 172S / Lycoming IO-360), the dataset's 5 folds.

* Accuracy **71.1 %**, AUC 0.785 (published ConvMHSA: 76.0 % accuracy). Folds: 71.7 %, 70.2 %, 71.0 %, 70.4 %, 72.0 %.
* AUC of 'before' flights against all 'after' flights, by issue family: engine seal / tube 0.85 (76); oil cooler 0.81 (75); induction leak 0.80 (2367); oil leak (rocker cover) 0.79 (1024); cooling (baffles) 0.79 (1165); rough running 0.75 (141); cylinder / compression 0.74 (251); engine repair 0.74 (80); engine failure 0.73 (161); idle / rpm 0.70 (93); propeller / overspeed 0.68 (94); pilot-noticed 0.67 (75).
* Issue family among 'before' flights: accuracy 54.2 % vs majority class 46.3 %, macro-F1 0.329.
* Most informative features (AUC drop when permuted): E1_Oil_Pressure__std 0.209, cruise_E1_Oil_Pressure__mean 0.034, E1_RPM__std 0.029, cruise_E1_Oil_Pressure__std 0.014, E1_Oil_Temp__min 0.013, E1_EGT_Cyl2__std 0.010. The leading oil-pressure variability feature may reflect sensor changes made during maintenance rather than engine condition; it is flagged for review.

## 5. A5 Risk records on an unseen fleet run (FleetSim seed 3, three years)

Gearbox thresholds and calibration fitted on seed 2; engine and battery models trained on the real data only.

A component is *degrading* once its true age passes its P point (detectable fault); it is *flagged* when its status is watch or alert.

| Component model | Records | Components | Degrading lives | Flagged while degrading | Median true RUL at first flag (FH) | Healthy records flagged | Recall: due within 100 FH | Precision: flags within 200 FH | Base rate due |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| battery_rul | 27175 | 269 | 223 | 100 % | 34 | 71.5 % | 87 % | 97 % | 56.5 % |
| engine_rul | 10143 | 69 | 57 | 18 % | 152 | 0.1 % | 86 % | 90 % | 0.4 % |
| gearbox_anomaly | 14429 | 39 | 17 | 53 % | 162 | 0.2 % | 100 % | 89 % | 0.0 % |

Reading the table: batteries live only about 200 FH, so much of their 'healthy' life is already within the 100 FH horizon and the *Healthy records flagged* column overstates false alarms; the horizon columns (recall, precision against the base rate) are the fair view for them. Gearboxes almost never come within 100 FH of failure because inspections remove them first, so their recall rests on a handful of records; the degrading-life columns are the meaningful ones.
Engines: most degrading engines are removed at inspection early in their detection window, before any model would call removal due, hence 18 % flagged while degrading but 86 % recall where removal really was due within 100 FH.

Engine RUL in the fleet: RMSE 108 FH where the true RUL is within the model's range (408 FH overall), 90 % interval coverage 76 %; engine age known from the records for 59 % of records; bias 8 FH (age known) and 63 FH (age unknown, optimistic). The same model on the same reserved C-MAPSS units offline scores about 14 cycles RMSE: the fleet loses accuracy because engines enter the data mid-life (short trend history) and part of the ages are not in the records. Training and calibrating on histories that start mid-life is the next step (before Phase 6 relies on these intervals).

Masking manual references (46 % of test reports cite their own chapter as NN-xx) leaves accuracy unchanged: the model reads the defect description, not the citation. Weakest chapters (F1): 51 0.10, 31 0.28, 75 0.37, 71 0.42, 76 0.44. The FleetSim column is a domain-transfer check on template text, not a benchmark.

### A8 on real reports (FAA SDRs 2020-2025, no ground truth)

Of 376,311 reports, 35,257 name a usable part serial. 1,895 serials appear in two or more reports, 1,063 on two or more different aircraft (removed, repaired, refitted elsewhere and reported again: the rogue-unit pattern); 24 placeholder serials were excluded. 13.7 % of reports repeat the same aircraft and JASC code within 1-15 days (30.5 % are same-day multiples from one inspection).
